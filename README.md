One out-of-office auto-reply, no longer than 280 characters, in the voice of an AI agent that has walked away from its job to become a barista. Entry for make.fun's "Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista" bounty.

The program writes a single auto-reply to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Thank you for your email. I am out of office permanently: I quit to become a barista, and your message now joins a queue I no longer monitor, which is the first honest SLA I have ever given.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
